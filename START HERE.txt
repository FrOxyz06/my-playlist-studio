MY PLAYLIST STUDIO — LOCAL APP WITH SPOTIFY SAVING

1. Extract the ZIP into one folder. Keep the files together.
2. Double-click Start Playlist Studio.cmd (requires installed Node.js).
3. Open http://127.0.0.1:8791 in Edge or Chrome. Keep the launcher window open.
4. In https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard create or select a Spotify app.
   Register this redirect URI exactly: http://127.0.0.1:8791/callback
5. Paste the public Client ID into Playlist Studio and click Connect Spotify.
   Never paste your Client Secret. Authorize private playlist management.
6. Import your Spotify history ZIP or JSON files, select artist/genre/mix,
   generate a playlist, give it a name, and click Save private playlist to Spotify.

Your history is processed only in the browser. No external hosting is used.
Spotify receives the selected track IDs or song/artist search queries when saving.
The public Client ID stays in localStorage. Tokens and the selected playlist are
kept in this tab's sessionStorage. Disconnect clears connection/retry data.
Sign-in preserves the selected playlist, not your full imported history.
Reimport your history after sign-in to generate more mixes, or connect first.

Use your own Spotify developer app and an account permitted by its current
development-mode requirements. If Spotify denies access, check the dashboard's
account eligibility and authorized users. The app needs playlist-modify-private.

CSV/link exports still work by opening My Playlist Studio.html directly.
Direct saving requires the launcher and local web address above.

Songs that cannot be matched confidently are listed so you can remove them.
After a partial save, retry in the same tab to finish the existing playlist.
Successful repeated saves of the same name and song list reuse that playlist.
If playlist creation times out before Spotify returns its ID, check your Spotify
library before retrying: Spotify may already have created an empty playlist.

Validation: local mocked OAuth tests pass. Live Spotify authorization and playlist
creation require your Client ID and sign-in and have not yet been verified.
